## 2. The 15 Toughest Questions Judges Will Ask (and Current Weak Spots)

1. **Q1 (NABARD / Banker):** *"Why do we need this when RBI already mandates SHG-BLP norms and banks use JanSamarth / CBS?"* — **Weak Spot:** Current UI mentions JanSamarth as an external link only; doesn't show *how* we fix pre-portal rejection causes (missing MCP, wrong ticket sizing, Capex/WC mixing).
2. **Q2 (GLPC Official):** *"Bank Sakhis already enter data into LokOS. Why would they type everything again in your app?"* — **Weak Spot:** Solved conceptually via `lokos-adapter`, but the CSV sync is buried inside `RoleProfitDashboardScreen` instead of clearly framed in Trust/Integration.
3. **Q3 (Bank Branch Manager):** *"All numbers in your intake and 12-month sales table are self-typed. How can I trust them for credit appraisal?"* — **Weak Spot:** `verifyQuorumOtp` & `captureGeoProof` (`FinancePassportScreen`) are simulated 1-click buttons without showing passbook/cashbook triangulation or audit hashes.
4. **Q4 (IAS / Policy Judge):** *"Your problem statement is Target vs. Access. Where in your dashboard do you actually measure Target vs. Access?"* — **Weak Spot:** `CommandCentreScreen` shows a stage funnel, **not** Target vs. Access metrics (Time-to-Credit, Sanctioned vs Eligible `6× corpus` ticket size, rejection reasons, repeat linkage).
5. **Q5 (Banker):** *"Do you even know RBI's SHG-BLP first-dose drawing power rule and whether individual member accounts are required?"* — **Weak Spot:** Currently nowhere in the app does it compute `max(6 × corpus, ₹1.5L)` or flag member-SB demands as non-compliant!
6. **Q6 (Mentor):** *"If an SHG gets ₹1L MMUY or ₹2.15L Bank Linkage, how does your app help them graduate to ₹3L Dose 2 and ₹6L Dose 3?"* — **Weak Spot:** Repeat linkage progression (`Dose 1 -> Dose 2 -> Dose 3`) is absent.
7. **Q7 (Banker):** *"Why does clicking 'SHG Leader' in the top-right open a 'Sakhi CreditLink Profit Calculator'?"* — **Weak Spot:** Serious UX bug (`App.tsx:423`) — exposes internal startup P&L to an SHG leader!
8. **Q8 (UX Judge):** *"Can a rural Sakhi with Class 8 education in Anand use a portal with 'DSCR', 'Capex', 'LSP Revenue', and floating tilted cards on a ₹7,000 Android phone?"* — **Weak Spot:** Language toggle fails on Hindi, Gujarati coverage is <15%, jargon is untranslated, and `shg-*.jpg` images weigh `3.47 MB`.
9. **Q9 (Social Impact Judge):** *"Are '148 SHGs' and '₹78L Disbursed' on your landing page real pilot results?"* — **Weak Spot:** Currently displayed without any `"Simulated / Illustrative"` label (`LandingPage.tsx:165-168, 402-405`) — **instant credibility loss if a judge thinks we faked traction!**
10. **Q10 (Legal / Tech Official):** *"You collect Aadhaar-linked KYC, bank passbooks, and group financials. Are you compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023?"* — **Weak Spot:** Consent records exist (`FinancePassportScreen`), but there is no explicit DPDP Act 2023 notice, data retention limit, or consent revocation toggle.
11. **Q11 (Banker):** *"What happens when a branch manager rejects or holds a file for 30 days?"* — **Weak Spot:** No standardized rejection reason codes, no SLA timer, and no automatic escalation to LDM / BLBC / DMMU.
12. **Q12 (Rural Tech Judge):** *"What happens to your web app in a village with 2G/zero internet?"* — **Weak Spot:** No `localStorage` draft saving, no offline indicator/queue, and external Google Fonts + `560 KB` JS bundle.
13. **Q13 (Business Mentor):** *"Your B2G + Bank LSP model sounds neat—can you legally charge banks per loan under RBI Digital Lending Directions without creating steering bias?"* — **Weak Spot:** Must clearly cite flat fee regardless of lender + zero borrower fee + no LSP interference in credit decision.
14. **Q14 (Academics):** *"Your Offer Comparison shows 3 banks, yet SBI/BoB/BGGB all show nearly identical product structures. Where is real differentiation?"* — **Weak Spot:** Needs real differentiators (turnaround SLA, collateral/stamp waiver, corpus multiplier, branch distance).
15. **Q15 (Judge):** *"What happens right now if I refresh the browser during your live demo or if the venue Wi-Fi drops?"* — **Weak Spot:** All newly entered SHG data disappears on F5 (`useState` only), and there is no **Reset Demo** button!
